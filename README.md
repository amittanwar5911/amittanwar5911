## 📡 Core ECE & Communication Systems

Alongside software development, I work on projects related to wireless communication, signal processing, and communication-system modelling.

### 📶 [AFDM-ISAC Simulation](https://github.com/amittanwar5911/AFDM-ISAC-Simulation)

A MATLAB simulation of an Affine Frequency Division Multiplexing (AFDM) waveform for integrated sensing and communication.

- Wireless communication and radar sensing using a shared waveform
- BER analysis under different SNR and Doppler conditions
- Pilot-aided channel estimation and equalization
- Range-Doppler processing and target detection

**Areas:** AFDM, ISAC, MATLAB/Simulink, signal processing, radar sensing

### 🧠 [Neural Network-Based Channel Estimation for DCO-OFDM](https://github.com/amittanwar5911/Neural-Network-based-channel-estimation-for-DCO-OFDM)

A neural-network-based approach to channel estimation for DC-biased optical OFDM systems used in visible light communication.

- QPSK modulation and Hermitian symmetry
- Multipath channel modelling with AWGN
- Dense neural networks with ReLU activation
- Channel-estimation evaluation and training-loss analysis

**Areas:** DCO-OFDM, visible light communication, deep learning, channel estimation
